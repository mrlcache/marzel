02 / why it exists

Built for the moment a small draft becomes a real project.

Marzel is designed for manuscripts that have outgrown scattered documents. Check a character detail, move between chapters and keep the information around the page close to the sentence.

The goal is not another system to learn. It is one quiet place where the manuscript, the page around it and the information it depends on can stay close.

Made for writers who want to stay with the work.
